# Map and adjacency: please check on the physical board

The in-game map (data/mapGeometry.json) is generated by tools/buildMap.py
and tools/optimizeMap.py so that its borders match the rules' adjacency
data (data/territories.json). It now matches on all but 10 borders. Each
one is either a limit of the generated map or an error in the adjacency
data, and only the physical board can say which.

## Likely a data mix-up: Wind Pass and Wind Pass North

The map places Wind Pass directly west of the Polar Sink and Wind Pass
North below it (south-west). With that geography, these data entries look
swapped between the two:

| Rules data says | The map draws instead |
|---|---|
| Wind Pass North touches The Great Flat | Wind Pass touches The Great Flat |
| Wind Pass touches Cielago North | Wind Pass North touches Cielago North |
| Wind Pass North touches Arsunt | (neither touches Arsunt; Arsunt is north of the Polar Sink) |

Question: on the board, which of the two touches The Great Flat, which
touches Cielago North, and does either touch Arsunt?

## Borders the map draws that the data says don't exist

1. False Wall South and Pasty Mesa
2. Pasty Mesa and South Mesa
3. Plastic Basin and The Great Flat
4. Rim Wall West and Sihaya Ridge
5. Rock Outcroppings and Tsimpo

For each: do the two territories share a border on the board (a line,
not just a corner point)?

Once answered, the data gets corrected where it's wrong, the optimiser
reruns, and the map redraws itself to match.
