# Faction ability audit

Every base-six ability from docs/faction-reference.md (GF9 2019 rulebook),
checked against the engine. Advanced rules are always on.

Status: LIVE = implemented and tested. BLOCKED = needs the board's storm
sector data. NOT YET = not built (reason given).

## Atreides
- LIVE: sees each treachery card before bidding
- LIVE: sees the top Spice card (house rule: from the end of the Spice Blow)
- LIVE: battle Prescience (one element), also in an ally's battles
- LIVE (advanced): Kwisatz Haderach after 7 forces lost; +2, one territory a turn
- LIVE (advanced): a leader carrying the Kwisatz Haderach cannot turn traitor

## Harkonnen
- LIVE: keeps all 4 traitors (usable for an ally too)
- LIVE: 2 starting cards, a free card with every purchase, hand limit 8
- LIVE (advanced): captured leaders (kill for 2 spice, or keep and use once)

## Emperor
- LIVE: receives other factions' treachery card payments
- LIVE (advanced): 5 Sardaukar, worth 2 (1 against the Fremen); 1 revived a turn
- LIVE (alliance): may pay for up to 3 extra revivals for the ally

## Fremen
- LIVE: chooses starting split between Sietch Tabr, False Wall South, False Wall West
- LIVE: free shipment onto the Great Flat or within two territories of it
- LIVE: moves 2 territories (3 with ornithopters)
- LIVE: never eaten by worms (nor the ally); may ride the worm after the Nexus
- LIVE: special victory (sietches held or empty, no Harkonnen/Atreides/Emperor in Tuek's)
- LIVE (advanced): controls the storm with the Storm cards (previews the next)
- LIVE (advanced): 3 Fedaykin, worth 2
- LIVE (advanced): forces fight at full strength without spice
- LIVE (alliance): the ally revives 3 forces free
- BLOCKED (advanced): storm losses halved

## Spacing Guild
- LIVE: receives other factions' shipping payments
- LIVE: ships at half price (rounded up); so does the ally
- LIVE: special victory if nobody has won by the end of the last turn (shared with ally)
- LIVE (advanced): may ship planet to planet, or back to reserves at 1 spice per 2
- LIVE (advanced): may act at any point in the Shipment and Movement order (the AI acts last)

## Bene Gesserit
- LIVE: secret Prediction (faction and turn; not the Fremen or Guild special wins)
- LIVE: Spiritual Advisors: 1 free force to the Polar Sink whenever another faction ships from off-planet
- LIVE: the Voice (play / don't play a card category), also in an ally's battles
- LIVE (advanced): always receives CHOAM Charity
- LIVE (advanced): any worthless card can be used as a Karama
- NOT YET (advanced): advisors as non-combatant coexisting forces, and the
  flip rules between advisors and fighters. A substantial rules system of
  its own; today every Bene Gesserit force is a fighter.

## All factions
- LIVE: Karama cancels the Voice, Prescience, or a Harkonnen capture
- NOT YET (advanced): each faction's once-per-game Karama power (GF9 wording disputed)
- BLOCKED: storm damage, Weather Control, Family Atomics

## Advanced game rules (always on)
- LIVE: spice-backed battle strength (unbacked forces count half)
- LIVE: double Spice Blow (two piles)
- LIVE: increased spice flow from strongholds
- LIVE: Storm cards controlled by the Fremen after the first storm
- LIVE: all faction advanced abilities above except those marked
